Ride Statistics

No connection to the bike computer.

TotalTourTrip Ride
Moving time
Time in which the speed sensor delivers distance, including coasting and cruise. Time without a connected speed sensor is never counted.
Distance without sensor
Distance the wheel counter advanced while the bike computer was off or the sensor disconnected. It is part of the distance, but not of any average — there is no time for it.
Averages
Distance with sensor divided by moving time; “incl. stops” adds short stops, “incl. breaks” adds stops longer than 2 min as well. “Ride mode only” leaves out cruise / free-ride distance and time (Pause button).
Ride
The ride session started with the Start button and ended with a long press on it. After the end it keeps showing the finished ride until the next start.
